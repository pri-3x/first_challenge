ERC-20 Workshop

Claim workshop tokens, approve the collector for the exact required amount, then let the collector pull those tokens with transferFrom.

  1. Call claim() on WorkshopToken. That account receives 100 WORK.
  2. Call approve(collector, 25000000000000000000) on WorkshopToken. That raw value is exactly 25 tokens.
  3. Call collect() on Collector. The collector pulls 25 WORK from you.

A direct transfer, an approval of 25, or an approval of any other amount leaves the challenge unsolved.
